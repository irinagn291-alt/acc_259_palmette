Palmette is a savings garden for people who log goals by hand, without a bank link. You plant a vine, pour cash onto its leader, then pinch the leader so wood records the target.

• Plant a vine with a name and a target
• Pour cash onto the leader to lengthen waiting flush
• Pinch the leader to lock waiting cash into wood
• Retract peels waiting cash only. Wood and beads stay
• Node beads write at 25, 50, 75, and 100 percent of that vine’s target, only after a pinch
• Garden shows hatched wood, dashed waiting, and beads on the canes
• Analytics counts node beads, ripe vines, and planted vines
• Pinch, then a node explains why waiting cash never beads
• Settings holds Contact Palmette, Re-run onboarding, and Reset all data
• Everything stays on this device. There is no bank link.

No accounts, purchases, or cloud sync.
